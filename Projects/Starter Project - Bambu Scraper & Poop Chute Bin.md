# 🛠️ Project: Bambu Scraper & Poop Chute Bin

The two essential prints every new Bambu owner should print immediately upon unboxing.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
* **Part 1: The Bambu Scraper Handle**
  * *Purpose:* Securely mounts the razor-thin metal scraper blade included in the Bambu X2D accessory box. Allows safe removal of primed purge lines and stuck brims without scratching the PEI sheet.
  * *Model Source:* Pre-loaded on printer internal flash memory and available on [MakerWorld: Official Bambu Scraper](https://makerworld.com/en/models/12702#profileId-13835).
* **Part 2: Rear Purge (Poop) Chute Bin**
  * *Purpose:* Snaps onto or sits directly beneath the printer's rear purge chute to catch purged filament coils and prevent them from scattering behind your desk.
  * *Model Source:* [MakerWorld: Bambu Magnetic Poop Chute Bin](https://makerworld.com/en/search/models?keyword=bambu+poop+chute).

---

## ⚙️ Slicer Configuration

| **Layer Height** | 0.20 mm Standard | 0.28 mm Draft (saves time) |
| **Wall Loops** | 3 walls | 2 walls |
| **Infill** | 15% Gyroid | 10% Adaptive Cubic |

| **Brim** | None | Auto Brim (prevents corner lift) |
| **Hardware Needed**| 2x M3 counter-sunk screws (included in box) | 2x to 4x 6x2mm magnets (optional) |

---

## 💡 Notes & Lessons Learned
* *Tip on Scraper:* When tightening the screws on the metal blade, do not over-torque or the countersunk plastic holes may strip. Hand-tight is sufficient.
* *Tip on Poop Bin:* Ensure the bin does not block the lower rear exhaust fan if printing high-temperature materials like ASA.
